Access to the application

URP+ Mobile is designed for authorised programme personnel and institutional partners. To access current project information, users need a Microsoft 365 account with the appropriate permissions to the organisation's SharePoint resources.

The ability to install the app does not grant access to restricted project information. If a document does not open, contact the person responsible for SharePoint access to that resource.

Common questions

How do I sign in? Open the app with an internet connection and complete the Microsoft 365 sign-in requested by your organisation.

Why is a document unavailable? The associated SharePoint resource may require additional permission, or your organisation's authentication session may have expired.

Can I use the app offline? Information previously synchronised on the same device may be available offline. Live updates, linked documents and photo uploads require connectivity and appropriate SharePoint access.

How do I report a problem? Contact the email above and describe the relevant app screen. Remove personal or confidential details from screenshots before sending them.
