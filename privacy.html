About this application

URP+ Mobile is an iPhone application for authorised users working with information on Ukraine recovery subprojects. The app displays information and documents hosted in an organisation-managed Microsoft 365 and SharePoint environment. Access to those resources is controlled by the organisation that manages them.

For questions about this policy or the application's handling of information, contact volodymyr.aredov@undp.org.

Information the app processes

  • Authentication and access: Microsoft 365 sign-in sessions and the related cookies or tokens needed to access authorised SharePoint resources. Authentication is provided by Microsoft and governed by the applicable organisational account settings.
  • Project information: portfolio records, progress and risk information, project documents and, where available, work-related contact details contained in authorised SharePoint data sources.
  • Photographs: images selected by users for a monitoring visit, image filenames, capture dates where available, and the selected subproject. Photos are uploaded to the appropriate SharePoint folders when the user initiates an upload.
  • Locally cached content: previously retrieved application data may be saved on the device to enable limited offline use.

How information is used

The app uses this information to authenticate users, display authorised programme information, navigate documents, view photographs, upload monitoring photos and make previously synchronised data available when the device is offline. SharePoint and Microsoft 365 may process associated service, access and security logs under the organisation's settings and Microsoft's applicable terms.

Access and third-party services

Current project information and uploads are accessed through Microsoft 365 and SharePoint. Access depends on each user's permissions to the relevant resources. The app is distributed through Apple's App Store; Apple may process app installation, diagnostic or store-related information according to its own terms and privacy disclosures. External websites or documents opened from the app may be subject to their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Project records, uploaded photos and associated account information stored in Microsoft 365 or SharePoint follow the retention and deletion policies of the organisation managing those systems. The app developer cannot unilaterally remove records held by those organisations.

Previously synchronised information may remain on the user's device until the app's local data is cleared or the app is removed. Users should protect their devices and must not install the application on devices that are shared with unauthorised persons.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of information handled through the app, email volodymyr.aredov@undp.org. Requests involving organisational records may need to be referred to the organisation responsible for those records.

Security

The application relies on organisational Microsoft 365 authentication and SharePoint permissions for online resources. Previously synchronised information on a device should be treated as internal work information, even when the device is offline. Users should keep their devices secured and report lost or compromised devices through their organisation's usual process.

Updates to this policy

This page may be updated when the application's features, connected services or data-handling practices change. The effective date above will be revised when substantive changes are made.
